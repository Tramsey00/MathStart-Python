# MS6-I01: Source notes — comparable educational products

- **Access date:** 2026-09-26
- **Method:** only official primary pages were used. A statement is limited to the page content observed without registration unless otherwise stated. This is a UX pattern review, not an efficacy or feature comparison.

## Comparison dimensions

Catalogue and grade/skill hierarchy; entry/auth boundary; independent practice; mathematical input or interaction; feedback/help; visible progress; mobile/accessibility cues; and what is outside the MathStart MVP. No conclusion is drawn for an unavailable, authenticated, or unobserved screen.

## 1. Khan Academy

- **Official source:** <https://www.khanacademy.org/about>
- **Also checked:** <https://www.khanacademy.org/math>
- **Observed:** the official About page exposes sign-in/sign-up, a Math entry point, and describes personalised courses, assessments, practice and support at a product level.
- **Useful comparison question:** how should MathStart distinguish course/topic discovery, practice and support without treating generic product claims as interaction specifications?
- **Observation limits:** the public Math URL was reachable but did not yield readable page content in the inspection tool. No logged-in learner, exercise, answer, hint, reveal, or progress screen was inspected.
- **Not transferred:** teacher/parent features, pricing or efficacy claims, and any unobserved learner flow.

## 2. IXL Math

- **Official source:** <https://www.ixl.com/math>
- **Also checked:** <https://www.ixl.com/diagnostic> and the official public UK landing page <https://uk.ixl.com/diagnostic>
- **Observed:** the Math page exposes search plus sign-in/join controls and a public grade/subject hierarchy with skill links, including grades and algebra. In the original inspection, the `.com/diagnostic` URL redirected to a regional Maths catalogue rather than an observable diagnostic flow. A separate official public UK landing page was later observable and publicly describes a diagnostic, current proficiency information, and personalised next-step recommendations at a product-summary level.
- **Useful comparison question:** how can a public hierarchy make a grade/skill entry understandable while MathStart keeps its separate Topic and Skill meanings?
- **Observation limits:** no registered user, actual question, feedback, recommendation, authenticated diagnostic session/result UI, or progress view was inspected. The public landing-page statements do not establish the interaction or appearance of those closed screens.
- **Not transferred:** IXL skill counts, awards, membership, or inferred adaptive behavior.

## 3. Яндекс Образование / Яндекс Учебник entry point

- **Official source:** <https://education.yandex.ru/>
- **Also checked:** the direct official public Яндекс Учебник page <https://education.yandex.ru/uchebnik/main>
- **Observed:** the top-level official page exposes a programme catalogue, a personal-account entry point, and a linked Яндекс Учебник route. The direct public Учебник page describes school informatics and mathematics materials, interactive assignments for grades 1--4, and auto-checked activities at a product-summary level.
- **Useful comparison question:** how should a top-level education portal send a learner to a bounded school-mathematics flow while keeping account context visible?
- **Observation limits:** no authenticated cabinet, concrete assignment, learner math exercise, result, or progress screen was inspected. Public descriptions are not treated as evidence of those closed interfaces.
- **Not transferred:** any assumed Учебник task model, scoring or teacher workflow.

## 4. GeoGebra

- **Official source:** <https://www.geogebra.org/>
- **Observed:** the public page exposes math-resource categories, calculators, grades 4--12 resources, a Join Lesson action, and distinguishes unauthenticated use from account-based online saving/sharing.
- **Useful comparison question:** how can MathStart make mathematical exploration/input approachable while retaining server-side validation and a much narrower MVP subject scope?
- **Observation limits:** no created lesson, saved work, classroom flow, solver session, feedback or progress view was inspected. GeoGebra’s teacher/classroom material is out of scope for MathStart MVP.
- **Not transferred:** calculators, OCR/photo solver, geometry canvas, classroom/teacher controls, or broad subject coverage.

## Resulting neutral observations for MathStart

The sources justify studying public catalogue hierarchy, explicit sign-in boundaries, skill/topic labels, and mathematical interaction affordances. They do not justify copying an interaction, claiming a feature exists behind login, or expanding MathStart past its contract-led student MVP.
